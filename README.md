# Nuri

AI infrastructure for hospitality operations.

## Vision
Nuri is building AI-native systems for restaurants, cafés, hotels, and hospitality businesses.

The goal is to simplify operations, improve guest experience, automate repetitive workflows, and help hospitality businesses operate more intelligently.

## Areas of Exploration
- Hospitality AI agents
- Restaurant operations automation
- Guest communication systems
- AI-powered staff assistance
- Knowledge management
- Internal workflow tools
- Analytics and reporting
- Voice and chat interfaces

## Status
Early stage exploration and infrastructure setup.
